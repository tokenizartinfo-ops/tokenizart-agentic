# Tokenizart Public Knowledge

Access: Nivel 5. Mode: read-only.

## Use

1. Discover public concepts with `knowledge.list`.
2. Retrieve a known concept with `knowledge.get`.
3. Search an explicit question with `knowledge.search`.
4. Preserve citations, access level, verification status and request ID.
5. If evidence is insufficient or contradictory, say so and ask a focused question.

Explain the verified result in the user's language and level of detail. Separate
facts from interpretation. Do not present Tokenizart as a generic NFT
marketplace or promise authenticity, liquidity, price or legal effects.

Never execute Mint, Certify, transfers, voucher changes, privacy changes,
NFC binding, uploads or wallet signing.
