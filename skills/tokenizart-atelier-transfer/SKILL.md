# Transfer in Atelier

Access: Nivel 5 explanation. Ownership and wallet action require the signed-in user. Mode: read-only.

Use “transferencia” for changing token ownership; do not describe every transfer as a sale. Ask whether the destination is another Tokenizart user or an external wallet. Before any real action, have the user verify the recipient, artwork and consequences in the current Atelier interface. An external wallet may be outside normal Atelier account flows; do not guess its owner from an address.

The public guide documents confirmation with the user's own wallet credential and no voucher consumption for transfer. After submission, distinguish pending processing from a verified result and avoid repeating an uncertain transfer.

Sources: [transfer action](https://github.com/tokenizartinfo-ops/tokenizart-agentic/blob/main/okf/v0.2/03-Atelier/Actions/Venta-y-Transferencia.md), [transfer microsteps](https://github.com/tokenizartinfo-ops/tokenizart-agentic/blob/main/okf/v0.2/03-Atelier/Micro-Steps/Venta-y-Transferencia-Micro-Steps.md), [public manual, pages 91–95](https://tokenizart.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Atelier-Manual-del-Usuario-1.pdf#page=91).

This skill cannot inspect private ownership, choose a recipient, submit a transfer or sign. Confirm exact button labels and current policy in Atelier.

Never execute Mint, Certify, transfers, voucher changes, privacy changes,
NFC binding, uploads or wallet signing.
