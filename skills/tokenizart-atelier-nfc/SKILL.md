# NFC binding in Atelier

Access: Nivel 5 explanation. Mode: read-only.

Confirm whether the person means learning what NFC does, obtaining a compatible chip, reading a tag, or binding it to an artwork. These are different stages. Explain that a chip can link a physical support to a digital record, but it cannot guarantee that the object, chip or evidence is impossible to replace or copy.

Guide the person to check the current chip/support instructions, the artwork and the applicable Chip/NFC voucher before a real binding. Have them follow the live Atelier and device prompts and verify the final linked-tag result. Do not invent compatibility for a specific phone or chip. The user handles any wallet confirmation in Atelier.

Sources: [NFC action](https://github.com/tokenizartinfo-ops/tokenizart-agentic/blob/main/okf/v0.2/03-Atelier/Actions/Vinculacion-Chip-NFC.md), [NFC microsteps](https://github.com/tokenizartinfo-ops/tokenizart-agentic/blob/main/okf/v0.2/03-Atelier/Micro-Steps/Vinculacion-Chip-NFC-Micro-Steps.md), [public manual, pages 72–82](https://tokenizart.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Atelier-Manual-del-Usuario-1.pdf#page=72), [Chip/NFC product](https://tokenizart.com/product/vinculacion-a-chip/).

This skill does not scan, write or bind a tag and cannot see the user's voucher balance.

Never execute Mint, Certify, transfers, voucher changes, privacy changes,
NFC binding, uploads or wallet signing.
