# Data and Assets Policy

The repository-wide CC BY 4.0 grant applies only to public documentation and
OKF concepts authored by Tokenizart and explicitly included under that policy.

It does not automatically apply to:

- artwork or object images;
- artist, owner or user metadata;
- Certify evidence, expert reports or certificates;
- signatures, reverse-side images or private documentation;
- third-party text, media, logos or trademarks;
- Gallery resources whose owner later changes visibility.

Every distributed third-party resource must carry machine-readable rights
metadata such as `rights_holder`, `license`, `license_url`, `attribution` and
`usage_restrictions`. Absence of rights metadata means no additional reuse
permission is granted.

Public visibility is not equivalent to an open-content license. Consumers
must respect privacy, publicity, moral, copyright and database rights.
