# Commercial Paths for Integrators

Tokenizart separates open interoperability from hosted business execution.
People and agents can discover public contracts, verified knowledge and
read-only traceability without receiving rights to operate Atelier, vouchers,
wallets, NFC binding or owner data.

> Open interoperability. Attributable knowledge. Proprietary execution.
> Protected brand.

The machine-readable source for this guide is
[`tokenizart-commercial-integration-policy.v1.json`](../contracts/tokenizart-commercial-integration-policy.v1.json).

## Choose the appropriate mode

| Mode | Appropriate when | Activation | Brand treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Public read-only | You need public knowledge, public Gallery traceability, contracts, skills or Demo Atelier | Public license terms | Accurate nominative reference only |
| Attributed integration | Your site, application or agent presents Tokenizart-backed public results or consumes contracted public quotas | Signed commercial agreement | Visible Tokenizart attribution |
| Managed white label | You need a custom frontend, domain or workflow backed by Tokenizart-operated services | Signed commercial agreement | Written brand and attribution annex |
| Enterprise OEM | You need institutional scale, governance, support, quotas or an expressly agreed deployment model | Signed commercial agreement | OEM license and brand annex |

If the mode is unclear, treat it as **public read-only**. Open-source code and
open documentation never imply hosted execution, owner-data, white-label or
trademark rights.

## Vouchers, gas and service quotas

These are different layers:

- A **voucher** is a Tokenizart commercial entitlement for an eligible action.
- **Gas** is a blockchain infrastructure cost. Tokenizart may abstract or
  sponsor it under the applicable platform policy and service agreement.
- An **API or MCP quota** limits service consumption. It does not replace an
  action-specific voucher or grant permission to execute that action.

Standard public product options may be available through the
[Tokenizart Shop](https://tokenizart.com/es/shop/). White-label and OEM scopes
use a commercial quotation based on authorized scope, volume, infrastructure,
support, security and branding rights. This repository is not a standing price
offer.

## What Tokenizart operates

Unless a written agreement expressly says otherwise, Tokenizart operates the
proprietary backend, owner and consent controls, voucher ledger, transaction
abstraction and sponsorship policy, smart-contract integration and configured
NFC services.

An integrator may build its own presentation layer and publish authorized
public results. It remains responsible for that frontend, its content, user
claims, security and compliance obligations. A customer or institution remains
responsible for lawful instructions, content rights and required owner
permissions.

No party should claim authenticity, ownership, professional conclusions or
provenance beyond the evidence actually verified and exposed for that scope.

## Required agreement package

A managed white-label or enterprise OEM project must define, in writing:

1. services and authorized scope;
2. brand and attribution;
3. usage, vouchers and billing;
4. data, privacy and owner permissions;
5. security, incidents and audit;
6. support, service levels and maintenance;
7. term, suspension, exit and portability.

Commercial contact: `tokenizart.info@gmail.com`.

## Public safety boundary

This public distribution does not grant Mint, Certify, transfer, voucher or
privacy mutation, NFC binding, uploads, wallet signing, owner context or
Gestion administration. Those capabilities require separate identity,
consent, scope, entitlement, audit and release controls.
