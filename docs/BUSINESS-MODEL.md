# Open Edge, Proprietary Core

Tokenizart opens the interfaces that help people and agents discover,
understand and integrate the platform. It keeps execution, operational policy
and sensitive infrastructure inside the hosted Tokenizart core.

> Open interoperability. Attributable knowledge. Proprietary execution.
> Protected brand.

## Open edge

- Official CLI and client examples.
- Public MCP contracts and schemas.
- Public Agent Skills.
- Verified public knowledge in OKF.
- Public blockchain addresses and read interfaces.

## Hosted core

- Atelier and Gestion backend.
- Owner context and consent enforcement.
- Voucher issuance, accounting and consumption.
- Transaction abstraction and sponsorship.
- Configured NFC products and encoding.
- Mint, Certify, transfer and other business mutations.

## Revenue model

Revenue can come from Shop vouchers, Starter Kits, configured NFC chips,
hosted action execution, API/MCP plans, professional services, enterprise
integrations, support/SLA and white-label/OEM agreements.

A customer may present its Tokenizart-backed works and provenance on its own
site. The customer gains a flexible frontend; Tokenizart remains the trusted
backend and execution infrastructure.
